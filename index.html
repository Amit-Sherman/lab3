Coin Dash

Collect yellow coins and clear five levels before time runs out. Each level adds new enemies: red bouncers, violet chasers, mint coin guards and orange dashers that flash before they charge. You have three lives. Grab power-ups for speed, a shield or extra time. Press T to race a friend.

1P move: arrow keys / WASD · 2P: P1 (green) WASD, P2 (blue) arrows · 1P/2P: T · Pause: P · Restart: R · Difficulty: 1 / 2 / 3 · Mute: M
